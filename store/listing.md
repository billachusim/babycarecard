## Full description (Play 4000 / App Store 4000)

Leaving your little one with a babysitter, nanny, grandparents or daycare? Baby Care Card puts everything they need to know on one simple card, so you're not writing notes on the fridge or answering texts all evening.

JUST SAY IT
Tap the microphone and talk for a minute: who your child is, what and when they eat, naps and bedtime, any medication, who to call and your pediatrician. Baby Care Card fills in the card for you. You check every field before anything saves.

EVERYTHING A SITTER NEEDS
• Feeding: formula, milk, meals, snacks and foods to avoid
• Naps, bedtime, diapering and soothing tips
• Medication with doses copied exactly as you say them
• Allergies front and centre
• Emergency contacts and your pediatrician

SHARE IT YOUR WAY
• Send it to the sitter in seconds
• Print a PDF for the fridge
• Show a QR code at the door

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your child's details stay on your phone. No account needed. If you use voice, only the text of what you said is sent to be organised, and nothing is kept.

ONE PAYMENT, NO SUBSCRIPTION
Free to use for one child, with 2 free voice fills. A one-off lifetime unlock adds unlimited children, unlimited voice fills and PDF export.

Baby Care Card doesn't give medical advice. Always check medicine names and doses against the label.

Also on the web at babycarecards.com.

## Privacy and Data safety
- Data collected: "Other user-generated content" (the voice transcript), processed ephemerally, not shared, not stored, used for app functionality only. No account, no analytics.
- Microphone and speech recognition: used only while you're speaking. The speech-to-text runs through the phone's own speech service.
